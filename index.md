# Welcome to Purdue IEEE EMBS

We are Purdue's student chapter of the **IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS)**.  
Our mission is to unite engineers and scientists passionate about medical technology, biomedical research, and healthcare innovation. Feel free to explore our website to learn about us and the projects that we are working on.

# Research
As a part of this club, we try to connect students with labs and professors offering research in areas of interest as well as conduct our own interdisciplinary research in fields of embedded systems, AI, and medical diagnostics. Explore this page to learn about opportunities for research within our club.

***
### **Our Research**
**Sleep Apnea Research**
- During the 2025-2026 academic year, one of our projects is a sleep apnea detection device that is used to continually monitor blood oxygen levels, breathing patterns, heart rythms, and brain activity whilst someone is sleeping in order to identify sleep apnea episodes that a patient might be having. However, we need to understand the biosignals we are getting from the device and how it connects to sleep apnea or the severity of an episode in order for our data to be of use. That is where researcher for this project come in. As a researcher, you will be looking into and understanding sleep apnea and the different ways that those biosignals relate to and signify sleep apnea as well as how those different biosignals show the severity of sleep apnea and physiological responses. To learn more about the sleep apnea project, visit our [**project page**]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/current-projects/sleep-apnea-kit/)


**Alzheimer’s / Parkinson’s Research**
- During the 2025-2026 academic year, one of our projects is a tri-modal home kit, which is meant to recognize and track indicators of Alzheimer’s / Parkinson’s progression. However, we need to consider what is generic behavior/movment/activity versus what is representative as disease progression, and that is where our research team will come in. The device will be responsible for gathering data and tracking different aspects such as tremors, gait, and eye-behavior, but the research team will be responsible for looking into how those different aspects can be representative of disease progression rather than simply generic behavior. As a researcher, you will be responsible for looking into, understanding, and defining measurable neurophysiological marker of Alzheimer’s / Parkinson’s. To learn more about the tri-modal home kit project, visit our [**project page**]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/current-projects/tri-modal-home-kit/)

- During the 2025-2026 academic year, one of our projects is project for a device for retinal imaging and in-ear biosensing in order to screen for Alzheimer's in a non-invasive manner. Normally, screening for Alzheimer's involves brain imaging and post-symptom cognitive testing, but our device will be able to detect neural and vascular biomarkers through the ears and eyes. However, we need to be able to understand how the biomarkers that we recieve relate to the disease that we are attempting to screen for. As a researcher, you will be responsible for linking retinal/EEG biomarkers to Alzheimer’s pathology so that we are able to better use and understand the data that our device is giving us. Through research, we will be able to use the device data effictively screen for Alzheimer's by understanding how the different biomarkers that we are receiving are indicative or correlating with Alzheimer's. To learn more about the Alzheimer's retinal scan / hearable project, visit our [**project page**]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/current-projects/alzheimers-retinal-hearable/)

**Lung Cancer Research**
- During the 2025-2026 academic year, one of our projects is a VOC analyzer project meant to detect lung cancer based on what is exhaled into the device. However, we need a way to understand how what is exhaled can be indicating of lung cancer, which is where our researchers will come in. While our device may be able to detect different compounds, we need to be able to determine what combination of compounds are naturally occuring depending on situations and which cases are actually indicative of lung cancer. As a researcher, you will be looking into clinical lung cancer profiles in order to determine the correlation between different compounds and those profiles, so that our device will be able to accurately predict whether someone has lung cancer based on the results. To learn more about the VOC analyzer for lung cancer project, visit our [**project page**]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/current-projects/voc-analyzer/)

**Communications Research**
- During the 2025-2026 academic year, one of our projects is a multilingual communications app in which we are attempting to utlize AI in order to convert text to speech and speech to text within a singular language and across mutliple or differing languages. In order to accomplish this, we need our model to fully understand the translation, including different factors such as slang and cultural differences in a language. As we are going to be using this to facilitate generic communication as well as conveying important medical information to the users, whether they be doctors or patients, we need to ensure that our translations are precise and accurate so that misinformation is prevented. As a researcher for this project, you will be doing a deep dive into different languages and cultural difference within a language so that our translation and communication is accurate and effective. To learn more about the multilingual healthcare communication app project, visit our [**project page**]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/current-projects/multilingual-app/)
